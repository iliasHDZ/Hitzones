# Hitzones
This mod reimagines how hitboxes are visualized. Hitboxes are
shown as if the player's hitbox is the size of a point rather
than a rectangle. The player dies when its center point touches
the red hitzones and its center point can land on blue hitzones.

## Usage
You can make hitzones visible by pressing tilde (´). You can
change this hot key in the settings. You can also enable
it by default and on death.
